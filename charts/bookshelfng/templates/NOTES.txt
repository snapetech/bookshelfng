BookshelfNG is running as a single-replica StatefulSet with persistent storage.

Access the web interface through the Service:
  kubectl port-forward svc/{{ include "bookshelfng.fullname" . }} 8787:{{ .Values.service.port }}
  open http://localhost:8787

The release chart pins the Hardcover image digest produced by that release. To
select the Softcover profile, clear image.digest and set image.tag=softcover.
Catalog providers can also be changed from BookshelfNG Settings after install.
